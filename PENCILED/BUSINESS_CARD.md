**Status:** 🟡 BUILDING

---

## The Pitch

Contractors don't want to do bookkeeping. They want to know one thing: *"Am I making money on this job?"*

Penciled answers that question automatically.

**Snap. Track. Know.**
- Snap a receipt → auto-categorized, assigned to job
- Track job costs as they happen
- Know your real profit per job - no spreadsheets, no guessing

---

## Target Customer

- General contractors, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing
- $300K - $3M annual revenue
- 1-15 employees
- Currently using QuickBooks badly or shoeboxes

## Core Features (MVP)

1. **Receipt Snap** - Photo → OCR → categorized → assigned to job
2. **Job Tracking** - Create jobs, set estimates, track actual costs
3. **Job P&L** - Real-time profit/loss per job
4. **Bank Connection** - Auto-import transactions via Plaid
5. **Ask Penciled** - "How much did the Johnson job cost?"

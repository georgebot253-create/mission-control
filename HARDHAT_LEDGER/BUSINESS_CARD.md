## The Product

Contractors don't want to do bookkeeping. They want to know one thing: *"Am I making money on this job?"*

Hardhat Ledger answers that question automatically.

**Snap. Track. Know.**
- Snap a receipt → auto-categorized, assigned to job
- Track job costs as they happen  
- Know your real profit per job - no spreadsheets, no guessing

---

## Target Customer

- General contractors, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing
- $300K - $3M annual revenue
- 1-15 employees
- Currently using QuickBooks badly or shoeboxes
